All six sit in the rail, in the slot you described: pinned just above the divider that carries Settings, so they never scroll away and never move between pages.

Toggle the freshness to see the amber state. That state is the only time this line has something urgent to say, so it is the one worth judging the options on — an option that looks calm when fresh and still says nothing useful at three days old is not doing its job.

My read. D is the one I would ship. It answers the question you actually have — how old is this — in the first line, and keeps the timestamp underneath for when you want to be sure, so nothing is hidden behind a hover. A and E are quieter but make you hover for the date, which on a phone means it is simply unavailable. B states the date and makes you do the arithmetic. C is D with the two lines the wrong way round: the label earns less than the answer does. F is the most informative and the least calm; the bar is a second encoding of something the dot already says.
